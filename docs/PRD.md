# Project brief

## Problem
Job applications need something stronger than a plain CV. I want a site that shows who I am and my projects.

## Audience
Recruiters and hiring managers for support operations / support automation roles.

## Must have
- Warm, human design with my own photo (carry over from the current site)
- About me and my story (moving from customer support toward automation)
- Skills (no Zendesk or Jira listed; Claude is listed under Automation and AI)
- Projects with visible results
- Link to a public CV (no phone number or town)
- Contact method that is safe to make public
- Works well on phone and desktop

## Nice to have
- Project write-ups for automation work (e.g. job-application tracker)
- Fuller 2-page CV download
- Custom domain

## Constraints
- Stack: TanStack Start + shadcn/ui, deployed on Netlify (see `docs/tech-stack.md`)
- Developed in WSL2 Ubuntu 24 with Node 24
- I do not code, so changes must be small, explained and verifiable
- Cost: aim for free tier hosting. Confirm Netlify's current free plan limits before go-live.
- The existing site's content and look must survive the rebuild. Nothing is deleted from `main` until I approve.

## Done means
New site live on Netlify, matching the current warm design and content, passing the checks in `AGENTS.md`.
